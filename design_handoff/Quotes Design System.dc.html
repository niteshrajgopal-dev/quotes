00 · Assumptions

  • Recoleta Bold is a licensed font not available in this environment. The prototype renders Fraunces (SOFT 100) as a metric-similar stand-in; production loads Recoleta via next/font/local.
  • Product photography is not yet supplied. Cups are rendered as brand-coloured product stand-ins so the motion can be evaluated; drop zones are provided for venue and craft photography. Replace with cut-out renders on transparent backgrounds (WebP/AVIF, ≥1600px tall).
  • The reference video shows: a dark espresso canvas, oversized marquee headline behind a product row, a pinned centre product that scales up while four feature blocks reveal around it, beans parallaxing over a CTA band, and a compact pill navigation. The prototype reproduces this choreography with Quotes' identity rather than its look.
  • Prototype stack. GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger run for real in the prototype. Framer-Motion interactions (hover, tap, nav state) are represented with equivalent CSS transitions and documented for hand-off.

01 · Visual direction

A dark room with a warm lamp. Espresso canvases carry the story; cream pages give it air. The cup is always the protagonist and the type is the set.

Story surfacesEspresso and Black for hero, drinks, feature and footer. Product sits on these with a long soft shadow.
Editorial surfacesCream for statements, craft and locations. Oversized type, generous margins, restrained 6–8px radii.
Accent momentsLatte appears as full surface only once per page (feature finale). Otherwise: kickers, rules, full stops, sleeves.
Tonal layeringMocha is the bridge tone: marquee text on espresso, secondary copy on cream, mid-state backgrounds.

06 · Motion system

1. One continuous storySections overlap by a 28px seam and share scroll timelines; nothing stops dead. Every section either hands its product to the next or lets type run underneath it.
2. Depth by rateBackground 0.1–0.25× · marquee type 0.3× · copy 0.5× · product 1× · beans 1.5–2×. The eye reads speed as distance.
3. Weighted, never bouncyOnly power/expo curves. No elastic, back or spring on editorial motion. Scrub smoothing 0.6s gives inertia without lag.
4. Reveal, don't fadeType reveals through line masks; images through clip-path; products through translate + scale. Opacity only rides along, never alone.
Durations
micro 150–250ms · ui 250–400ms · editorial reveal 600–900ms · section transition 800–1400ms · loader ≤1500ms total
Easings
power2.out (hover, ui) · power3.out (entrances) · power4.inOut (state swaps, masks, bg colour) · expo.out (line reveals, hero) · none (scrub parallax)
Parallax distances (desktop · mobile · reduced)
product 360 · 200 · 90px · beans 520 · 290 · 130px · copy 120 · 66 · 30px · marquee 12 · 7 · 3% width. Global multiplier K = 1 · 0.55 · 0.25.
Pinning
Two pins per page maximum (Drinks 3.2vh, Feature 2.6vh of scroll; ×0.7 mobile, ×0.5 reduced). anticipatePin:1, scrub 0.6.
Reveal rules
Trigger at 85–90% viewport, once. Stagger 80–120ms, max 6 items. Line masks translate 110%. Clip masks inset from bottom.
Hover rules
Transform + colour only. Buttons scale 1.03; cards lift 6px; rows indent 12px. All ≤450ms. Tap scale .97, 150ms.
Reduced motion
K = 0.25, pins halved, hero copy stays opaque, no rotation, entrances become 400ms translate 12px + opacity. Honour prefers-reduced-motion via gsap.matchMedia.
Performance
Only transform, opacity, clip-path, background-color animate. Products are will-change:transform while pinned; grain overlay is a static texture stepped at 2fps.

GSAP in React. Each section calls useGSAP(() => {…}, {scope: ref}) (or gsap.context in useLayoutEffect) and returns the context's revert() on unmount. Timelines are built inside gsap.matchMedia() with three conditions: (min-width:900px), (max-width:899px), (prefers-reduced-motion:reduce), each choosing a distance multiplier K and pin length.

Motion tokens live in lib/animations/tokens.ts and mirror section 06: dur.micro / ui / reveal / section, ease.out2 / out3 / io4 / expo, depth.bg / type / copy / product / bean.

Framer Motion owns Header (compact variant), buttons (whileHover / whileTap), menu rows, location cards, and route transitions (AnimatePresence, 400ms cream wipe). It never touches scroll-driven layers.

Mobile. Below 900px: hero stacks (cup above copy at 60vw), drinks side-list becomes a horizontal price strip under the cup, feature ingredients render as a 2-column grid beneath the product, pins shorten to 0.7×, parallax K = 0.55, horizontal marquee movement is disabled to avoid overflow.
